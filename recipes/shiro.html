Shiro

Description

Shiro is one of Ethiopia's most beloved comfort foods — a smooth, spiced stew made from ground chickpea or broad bean flour. It's simmered with onions, garlic, and berbere until it turns into a thick, velvety sauce. Simple, affordable, and deeply satisfying, Shiro is a staple in Ethiopian households and is especially popular during fasting periods since it's naturally vegan.

Despite its short ingredient list, Shiro is all about technique — getting the right consistency and balance of spice takes practice, and every household has its own version passed down through generations. It's typically served with injera, torn and used to scoop up the stew.
