Shiro

Description

Shiro is one of Ethiopia's most beloved comfort foods — a smooth, spiced stew made from ground chickpea or broad bean flour. It's simmered with onions, garlic, and berbere until it turns into a thick, velvety sauce. Simple, affordable, and deeply satisfying, Shiro is a staple in Ethiopian households and is especially popular during fasting periods since it's naturally vegan.

Despite its short ingredient list, Shiro is all about technique — getting the right consistency and balance of spice takes practice, and every household has its own version passed down through generations. It's typically served with injera, torn and used to scoop up the stew.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup shiro powder (ground chickpea/broad bean flour with spices)
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp berbere spice
  • 3 tbsp niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter) or vegetable oil
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped (or 2 tbsp tomato paste)
  • 3-4 cups water
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 green chili, sliced (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the niter kibbeh or oil in a pot over medium heat, then add the chopped onions.
  2. Cook the onions until soft and translucent, about 8-10 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger, cooking for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the berbere spice and cook for 1 minute to release its flavor.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes (or tomato paste) and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  6. Pour in the water and bring to a gentle boil.
  7. Slowly whisk in the shiro powder, a little at a time, to avoid lumps.
  8. Reduce heat to low and let it simmer, stirring frequently, for 10-15 minutes until thick and smooth.
  9. Season with salt to taste, top with sliced green chili if using, and serve hot with injera.
