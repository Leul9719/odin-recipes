Kitfo

Description

Kitfo is a distinctive Ethiopian dish made from finely minced raw or lightly warmed beef, seasoned generously with mitmita (a fiery chili spice blend) and niter kibbeh. Often compared to steak tartare, it's prized for its rich, buttery texture and bold heat. Traditionally, the beef is minced by hand with a knife rather than ground, which gives it a distinct texture different from other minced meat dishes.

Kitfo is usually served with ayib (a mild, crumbly Ethiopian cottage cheese) and gomen (collard greens) to balance out the spice, alongside injera. It's a dish closely tied to celebration — commonly served at weddings and special gatherings in the Gurage and Kembata communities where it originates, though it's beloved across Ethiopia today.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean beef tenderloin, finely minced
  • 3-4 tbsp niter kibbeh (Ethiopian spiced butter), melted
  • 1-2 tbsp mitmita (Ethiopian chili spice blend)
  • 1 tsp korerima (Ethiopian cardamom), ground
  • Salt, to taste
  • Ayib (Ethiopian cottage cheese), for serving
  • Gomen (sautéed collard greens), for serving
  • Injera, for serving
